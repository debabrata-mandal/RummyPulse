Safe Play & Game Points Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

RummyPulse is a score-recording utility. It must not be used to organise, manage, calculate, or settle games involving money or anything of value.

Game Points

Game Points (“GP”) and Board Points are virtual scoring measurements used to compare game results. They:

  • have no monetary or redeemable value;
  • cannot be purchased, sold, deposited, withdrawn, transferred between accounts, or exchanged;
  • cannot be redeemed for money, goods, services, entry, prizes, or rewards; and
  • do not form a wallet, stored-value account, or transferable balance.

The Game Point Factor is a dimensionless scoring multiplier. It is not a currency conversion rate. Board Points are a synthetic per-game calculation used to keep the Game Point result mathematically balanced; the Board is not a person, recipient, fund, or account.

Permitted use

You may use RummyPulse to record players, rounds and scores; calculate virtual Game Point results; review completed games; and view non-monetary performance history.

Prohibited use

You must not use RummyPulse for wagering, stakes, entry fees, cash games, payments, debts, winnings, payouts, goods, services, prizes, rewards, or any other item of value. Renaming a real-world amount as Game Points, chips, credits, or another virtual unit does not make that use acceptable.

Acceptance and enforcement

After signing in, you must accept the current version of this policy before entering the app. RummyPulse stores the accepted policy version and a server timestamp for your safe-play confirmation. A first-time confirmation requires an internet connection. If this policy changes materially, the app may require you to confirm the new version.

Declining the policy signs you out. Accounts may be restricted or removed if the app is used in violation of these rules.

Privacy and account deletion

For information about the confirmation records and other data handled by RummyPulse, read the Privacy Policy. You can also review the account-deletion instructions.
