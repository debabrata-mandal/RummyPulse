Delete your RummyPulse account

Updated: September 13, 2026

You can permanently delete your RummyPulse account from the app without administrator approval. Open the navigation drawer, tap Delete account, review the explanation, and confirm with your Google account.

If you cannot use the app

Send a deletion request from the same email address used to sign in to RummyPulse. This lets us verify that the request belongs to you.

Request account deletion

If you want a name-only record from an older game reviewed, include the specific game ID. We will not identify or change an older player solely by matching a name because that could affect another person with the same name.

What is deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account and its Google Sign-In association.
  • Your RummyPulse profile, including your email address, display name, profile-photo URL, role, Firebase UID, and account timestamps.
  • Your personal player statistics and game-view requests or approvals.
  • Your account identifiers from active and approved games and score-history records.

What remains

Shared games are not deleted because they contain other players’ scores and settlement records. Your player position and scores remain under the name Deleted player, without a Firebase UID or account link. Player counts, scores, GST, settlements, timestamps, and aggregate reports remain unchanged.
